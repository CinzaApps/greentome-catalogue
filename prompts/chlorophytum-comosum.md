## Prompt

```
Studio product photograph of a single Spider plant (Chlorophytum comosum) houseplant, a young
healthy indoor specimen, shot for a plant nursery catalogue. A dense rosette of about 30 arching,
narrow, grass-like leaves, 20 to 35 cm long and 1.5 to 2.5 cm wide, mid-green with one broad
creamy-white stripe down the centre of each, curving outward and down over the rim on all sides. Two
or three thin pale stems arch out beside the pot, each ending in a small baby plantlet of a few
short leaves and carrying one or two tiny white six-petalled star-shaped flowers; the plantlets hang
no lower than the middle of the pot. The pot is small in the frame, exactly the size, shape and
position of the straight-sided pot in the reference images, about a quarter of the image width; the
plant is scaled to fit that pot, not the pot to the plant. Slight natural asymmetry, leaves not all
the same size, every leaf healthy and undamaged.

Pot: a plain matte unglazed terracotta cylinder pot with natural clay texture, no decoration, no
saucer, opaque, exterior view only.

Photography: soft, even, shadowless diffused light, slightly brighter from the upper left, as in a
professional plant catalogue. No lighting equipment, light stands, softboxes or studio gear visible
anywhere in the frame. Everything in sharp focus from the front leaf to the back, no bokeh, no blur.
Neutral true-to-life colour, natural greens, no warm or yellow cast, no oversaturation, matte leaf
surfaces with only a faint natural sheen. Straight-on eye-level view, no lens distortion.

Composition: square image. Centred, pot base near the bottom edge, the whole plant visible with a
small margin at the top. One small soft contact shadow directly under the pot. The background is a
single completely uniform flat warm off-white colour (#F4F0E6) from edge to edge, like a cut-out on
a plain backdrop: no floor, no horizon line, no curve, no wall, no shading, no vignette, no
gradient, no texture.

Match the pot, pot colour, background colour, lighting, camera angle and framing of the attached
reference images exactly. Only the plant changes; do not copy the reference plants' leaf shape,
pattern or flowers.

Do not include: illustration, painting, cartoon or 3D-render look, plastic or artificial-looking
leaves or flowers, cinematic lighting, golden hour, vignette, shallow depth of field, browned or
damaged leaves, text, labels, people, hands, other plants, props, a frame, a watermark.
```
